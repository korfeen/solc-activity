Privacy Policy

SOLC Puzzle, the Sleepy Ogre Leisure Club's Discord Activity. Effective 7 October 2026.

In short: SOLC Puzzle only needs to know who you are on Discord so it can show your name in a puzzle race. It keeps the current race (who's racing, their moves and times) until the next race replaces it. It doesn't sell, share or analyse your data, and it shows no ads.

Who runs it

SOLC Puzzle is a free, non-commercial game made by members of the Sleepy Ogre Leisure Club, a World of Warcraft guild. The code is public at github.com/korfeen/solc-activity.

What we collect, and why

  • Your Discord account basics: your user ID, username or display name, and avatar. When you start the Activity, Discord asks you to allow this (the identify permission). We use it to show you in the player list and on race results.
  • Race data: who took part in a race, the picture and puzzle that was raced, each racer's moves and finish times, and who gave up. This is what makes races, live boards and replays work.
  • A temporary login token from Discord, used only to confirm who you are when you join a race room. It isn't stored.

We don't collect your email, your messages, your server list or anything else from your Discord account.

How long it's kept

  • Each Activity session has its own race room. It keeps only the latest race; starting a new race replaces it.
  • Your name and avatar are held only while you're connected to the room.
  • Your best practice times are saved in your own browser (local storage) and never leave your device.

Who else handles it

  • Discord runs the Activity inside its app and handles the login. Their privacy policy applies to your use of Discord: discord.com/privacy.
  • Cloudflare hosts the Activity and its race rooms. As a hosting provider, it processes technical data such as IP addresses to deliver the service: cloudflare.com/privacypolicy.

We don't sell your data or share it with anyone else, and we don't use analytics or advertising.

Your choices

  • You can stop using the Activity at any time, and remove its access in Discord under User Settings → Authorized Apps.
  • To ask what we hold about you, or to have a race deleted, open an issue at github.com/korfeen/solc-activity/issues or ask a guild officer in the Sleepy Ogre Leisure Club Discord.

Children

SOLC Puzzle is for people who meet Discord's minimum age in their country. We don't knowingly collect data from anyone younger.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and its date. Big changes will also be announced in the guild's Discord.

See also the Terms of Service. SOLC Puzzle isn't affiliated with Discord or Blizzard Entertainment.
